Where you're staying

Camden base

Camden works well for this trip. The market is on your doorstep, so you can dip in more than once instead of cramming it into one visit, and the Northern line's two branches both pass through Camden Town, putting Soho, Bloomsbury, King's Cross and Old Street on a direct ride. The Overground from Camden Road runs straight to Dalston, which makes the east London charity-shop day easy.

Camden TownNorthern (both branches)Main station. Charing Cross branch to Goodge St, Tottenham Court Rd, Leicester Sq. Bank branch to Euston, King's Cross, Angel, Old Street.
Chalk Farm / Mornington CrescentNorthernQuieter alternatives when Camden Town is crowded, which is most likely on weekend afternoons.

Which station is closest depends on where exactly the Airbnb is. Camden Road is about a 10 minute walk east of Camden Town.

King's Cross → Bloomsbury → Covent Garden → Soho

Museum #1 + central vintage
King's Cross St PancrasTottenham Court RoadLeicester Square
Breakfast
Northern line two stops to King's Cross for breakfast at Dishoom King's Cross (your friend's pick; the bacon naan roll is the move).
Morning
Next door at Coal Drops Yard, browse Boutique by Shelter (Unit EV8), a curated charity boutique. Then walk south through Bloomsbury (about 20 minutes), stopping at Silk Road Charity Bazaar (67 Marchmont St) for Central Asian textiles and crafts, on to the British Museum (free). Pick two or three galleries rather than trying to see it all.
Afternoon
Walk south to Covent Garden: Rokit (42 Shelton St) for costume-y accessories, cowboy boots, military gear. Then Soho: Reign Vintage (12 Berwick St) and a wander down Berwick Street. Coffee at Monmouth on Monmouth Street.
Evening
Bocca di Lupo (Soho) for Italian small plates; half portions keep it reasonable. Northern line home direct from Leicester Square.

Tuesday29Sep

Camden Market → London Zoo → Regent's Park

Your Camden day, all on foot
On foot from the AirbnbCamden Town
10:00
Camden Market opens 10am and weekday mornings are the calmest. Start at the Stables Market, where most of the vintage clothing, antiques and records are, then Camden Lock and the High Street shops (Rokit has a Camden branch too). Early lunch from the market food stalls.
13:00
Walk about 15 to 20 minutes along the canal or Parkway to ZSL London Zoo on the north edge of Regent's Park. Open 10am to 5pm this time of year, last entry 4pm. Adult tickets run about £28 to £35 online, cheaper if bought before 10am on the day, so buy on your phone over breakfast.
17:00
If there's light left, walk south through Regent's Park for Frieze Sculpture (free, outdoor, runs to 1 Nov). Sunset is around 6:45pm.
Evening
Back to Camden or Primrose Hill for dinner (see the list below).

Thursday1Oct

South Kensington, Chelsea & Knightsbridge, dinner in Fitzrovia

Museum #2 + designer charity shops + Harrods + the nice meal
South KensingtonSloane SquareKnightsbridgeOxford Circus
Morning
Northern line to Leicester Square or King's Cross, then Piccadilly line to South Kensington. V&A (free, opens 10:00): the Fashion gallery plus the new free show The Burberry Trench: Crafting an Icon (from 21 Sep). Coffee and a pastry in the V&A café.
Afternoon
Octavia Foundation (3 Bute St, steps from the station) for high-end donated labels. Walk or bus down to Chelsea: British Red Cross (69-71 Old Church St, big and well known for designer finds), then work along the King's Road, which has a run of charity shops.
~16:00
Walk up Sloane Street to Harrods (87-135 Brompton Rd; Mon to Sat 10am to 9pm) for gifts, aiming to arrive by about 4pm. The Food Halls are the easy win: tea and biscuits, the Chocolate Hall, and the Roastery & Bake Hall, all in packaging that travels well. Doing Harrods last means you only carry the bags as far as dinner. Leave by 5:30pm to make the 6pm booking. Note that the UK no longer offers VAT refunds to tourists, so the price on the tag is what you pay.
18:00
Booked Chishuru (3 Great Titchfield St, Fitzrovia), table at 6pm. From Knightsbridge, Piccadilly line to Green Park, then Victoria line one stop to Oxford Circus, about 20 minutes door to door. One Michelin star, modern West African cooking from chef Joké Bakare, drawing on Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa food. Michelin singles out the banga fish stew with turbot. Home: walk about 10 minutes to Goodge Street for the Northern line direct to Camden.

  • Pahli HillBombay-inspired Indian near Chishuru, basement bar
  • Bar KinkyInventive cocktails beneath a dumpling restaurant

Friday2Oct

Holborn → Spitalfields → Brick Lane → Shoreditch

Museum #3 (small) + east-end vintage + fondue + cocktails
HolbornLiverpool StreetOld Street
Getting there
Northern line to Tottenham Court Road, then Central line one stop to Holborn.
Breakfast
Prufrock Coffee on Leather Lane, a short walk from Holborn.
10:00
Sir John Soane's Museum (13 Lincoln's Inn Fields), free, walk-in, Wed to Sun only. An architect's house crammed with antiquities, mirrors and hidden panels. An hour is plenty and it's the quirkiest museum in London.
Afternoon
Central line Holborn → Liverpool Street. Atika (55-59 Hanbury St), a multi-floor vintage department store in an old furniture factory. Old Spitalfields Market, then Brick Lane's vintage shops and House of Vintage (4 Cheshire St) for rare one-offs from 1920s gowns to 70s shirts. Lunch at Smoking Goat (64 Shoreditch High St), a Thai grill your friend recommended, or a salt beef bagel at Beigel Bake if you're hungry.
Dinner
Bistrot Walluc (40 Redchurch St, Mon to Sat from 6pm): a tiny fondue bistro that feels like a living room full of kitsch and vintage objects, sometimes with live music or card tricks. The cheese fondue set menu comes with bread, potatoes, salad and cheese or charcuterie (advertised at £24). Only about 30 covers, so book, and call a day ahead to confirm. Shoreditch High Street station is a few minutes away.
Late
Callooh Callay (65 Rivington St, about a 10 minute walk): the cocktail bar pick. Old Street station is a short walk, and the Northern line (Bank branch) runs direct back to Camden Town.

Getting around

Tube, Overground & bus

Paying

  • Tap in and out with a contactless card or phone. Each person needs their own card or device.
  • Always use the same card or device all day so the daily cap applies.
  • Zones 1 to 2: £3.60 peak, £3.10 off-peak per ride, capped at £8.90/day; weekly cap (Mon to Sun) £44.70.
  • Buses: £1.75 per ride, £5.25 daily cap. Tap in only.
  • Heathrow on the Piccadilly or Elizabeth line counts toward the cap. Heathrow Express does not.

From Camden

  • Northern, Charing Cross branch: Goodge St, Tottenham Court Rd (change for Central and Elizabeth), Leicester Sq (change for Piccadilly)
  • Northern, Bank branch: Euston, King's Cross, Angel, Old Street
  • Overground from Camden Road: Highbury & Islington, Dalston Kingsland
  • At King's Cross: Piccadilly (Heathrow, South Ken, Holborn); Hammersmith & City and Circle (Farringdon, Liverpool St, Ladbroke Grove)
  • Northern line trains alternate between branches, so check the destination on the platform board before boarding.
  • No tube strikes were scheduled for these dates when this was written. Check TfL on the day.
  • Sunset is around 6:30 to 6:50pm this week, so plan outdoor things earlier in the day.
